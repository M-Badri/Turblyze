# Reference Sources: Taylor-Green Vortex (Re = 1600)

1. **van Rees, W. M., Leonard, A., Pullin, D. I., & Koumoutsakos, P. (2011)**
   *A comparison of vortex methods and pseudospectral methods for the simulation of periodic vortical flows at high Reynolds numbers.*
   *Journal of Computational Physics*, 230(8), 2794-2805.
   $512^3$ dealiased pseudospectral reference data for incompressible Navier-Stokes at $\text{Re} = 1600$.

2. **International Workshop on High-Order CFD Methods (HiOCFD)**
   *Benchmark Problem C3.5: Direct Numerical Simulation of the Taylor-Green Vortex at Re = 1600.*
   Reference dataset: `spectral_Re1600_512.gdiag` ($512^3$ pseudo-spectral solution tracking time, kinetic energy, dissipation rate $-dE/dt$, and enstrophy).

3. **Brachet, M. E., Meiron, D. I., Orszag, S. A., Nickel, B. G., Morf, R. H., & Frisch, U. (1983)**
   *Small-scale structure of the Taylor–Green vortex.*
   *Journal of Fluid Mechanics*, 130, 411-452.
   Original derivation of the impermeable symmetry subspace $[0, \pi L]^3$ and transition to small-scale turbulence.

4. **DeBonis, J. R. (2013)**
   *Solutions of the Taylor-Green Vortex Problem Using High-Resolution Explicit Finite Difference Methods.*
   AIAA Paper 2013-0382, 51st AIAA Aerospace Sciences Meeting.
